Subject: Harbour concept — yacht application workflows for your feedback and consideration

Dear Immigration Team,

When I moved to Anguilla earlier this year, a few people suggested that I explore a solution to the difficulties brokers were experiencing with the manual paperwork associated with yacht registration and clearance.

Those conversations led me to develop Harbour, a concept for coordinating yacht-visit applications between customers, brokers, Immigration and Customs. Brokers have expressed interest in the platform, and I’m now sharing this early concept with your team for feedback and consideration.

I recognise that a successful solution would need the input and support of both Immigration and Customs. My intention is to help create a process that works for visiting customers, brokers and the Government of Anguilla, while preserving each department’s responsibilities and decision-making authority.

In my view, the experience of arriving by yacht should reflect Anguilla’s standing as a premium destination. A clear, efficient and welcoming administrative process is an important part of that experience.
