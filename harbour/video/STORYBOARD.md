The fictional North Star visit connects the Customs, Immigration and payment screens. Identity checks do not admit a person. Payment does not grant clearance or issue a permit. Receipt verification and issuance each require an explicit, separate review. The issued demo copy shown is not yet valid for its future visit dates; no active-clearance claim is made. The delivery event is a stored report, not a sent message.

Editorial rules: persistent Product demo label; fictional people, passport references, financial values and authority actions; no real personal data, live integration claims, tariff claims, endorsements or invented productivity figures. The example 225.00 XCD is not represented as a current government tariff.

Layout: independent opaque scenes with no overlapping sequence durations. Left text and callouts remain outside the screenshot frame. Whole panels use contain sizing and do not pan past field labels. Captions have their own bottom safe area. No screen overlay masks a record. On-screen text carries the complete story without sound.

Capture provenance: `public/stakeholder-captures.json`. Screens were captured through the browser automation API in a fresh, isolated local demo session from app revision 79f4043. A simulated payment, explicit receipt verification, explicit issuance and reported delivery created the visible states. The website source was not modified for the video.
