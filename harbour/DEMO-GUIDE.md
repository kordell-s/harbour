# Harbour stakeholder walkthrough

Use fictional information only. The demo holds records in browser memory and has no authority connection or legal validity. Reload or the confirmed Reset demo action restores the examples.

## Presentation sequence

1. Open **Broker portal**. Azure needs Customs clarification of tonnage and anchorages. Solstice is an incomplete draft. Sea Glass has sample inward outcomes, but no actual arrival reported and no universal approval.
2. Continue Solstice's draft or prepare an Azure amendment. Show vessel use, registered tonnage, registry port, owner/agent details, voyage information, planned activities and optional external reference. SailClear availability and actual requirements are explicitly unconfirmed. Insurance filenames are not a universal gate.
3. Review before submitting. Broker edits remain separate until confirmed. On resubmission earlier decisions remain in history and existing outcomes are flagged for review. This conservative demo policy needs stakeholder validation.
4. Open **demo officer review**. Select a specific process and record its applicability, outcome, reference, reason, and—where relevant—permit validity and public conditions. Only that process changes. This is an unauthenticated role simulation.
5. Use Sea Glass to record a cruising permission. Select Required and Recorded, enter a fictional reference and dates, and describe the limited scope. Marine-park permission requires current inward outcomes. Tax/licensing remains unconfirmed unless explicitly assessed; it does not block all arrivals.
6. In **Broker view**, record an actual arrival under Arrival and departure. With current inward outcomes, the coordination stage becomes Stay management. Record a sample receipt separately; specify the authority, purpose, amount, currency, assessment date and receipt. Payment does not issue a permit or clearance.
7. Start departure preparation. In the authority view record Customs outward clearance and Immigration departure review separately. Actual departure cannot be completed in the demo until the inward and outward records are current.
8. Open **Verification record**. Review each process's reference, applicability, scope and permit dates. There is no blanket Approved badge, no passenger identity data and no payment receipt disclosure. Static QR images from the previous hosted demo are no longer displayed.
9. Inspect **Activity**. Officer changes retain previous process records, cancellation requires a new reference to reissue, and broker-reported movements/receipts are labelled separately. Reset demo has an explicit discard confirmation.

## Confirm with stakeholders

The workflow is informed by published Anguilla sources, not certified by departments. Validate captain attendance, exact forms, private/charter applicability, current fees, permit scopes, IRD responsibilities, amendment rules and individual Immigration handling. Marine-park mooring permission is modelled; other activities such as fishing may need additional separately scoped processes.

See `PROCESS-RESEARCH.md` for source links and open questions. The coastal design and broker preparation flow remain intact. Authentication, secure documents, shared persistence, official issuance, integrations and live QR verification remain future work.

## Paper-to-digital document demonstration

1. Open Sea Glass, then **Open document pack**. Sea Glass has fictional inward records, useful for showing the outward-clearance branch.
2. On **Cruising application**, use **Use fictional example** or fill the grouped vessel, party and requested-permission fields. Save, review the saved snapshot, and submit the document request. These are separate from visit submission.
3. Open **Demo authority desk**. Record assessment `DEMO-A-1`, amount `25.50`, currency `XCD`, date `2027-01-08`, and a basis explaining this is a fictional manually assessed amount. This amount is only a UI demonstration, not a tariff quote.
4. Return to the pack. Link receipt `DEMO-R-1` for the same amount/currency and date. It initially shows unverified evidence.
5. At the authority desk, verify the receipt with a reason. Then issue `DEMO-C-1`, with issue date `2027-01-09`, place Road Bay, validity `2027-01-10` to `2027-01-16`, a sample scope and private reason. Trying to issue before receipt verification shows a blocking message.
6. Open **View copy**. The application, assessment and receipts are frozen in the copy. Print or download its standalone HTML version; open downloaded HTML in a browser to save as PDF. Record a sample delivery recipient; no message is sent.
7. Switch to **Outward clearance**. Shared details are reused. Reconcile crew/guest counts, construction and destination. Submit, then issue a separate clearance from the authority desk. This does not complete Immigration departure review.
8. Prepare amendments or use the process review to cancel. Earlier documents keep their content and show review/cancellation status; reissue requires a revised request and a new document number.

All values and document controls are fictional. The supplied real clearance scan's identities and signature were not imported. Requested and issued dates remain separate; current fee rules and calendar expiry conventions require stakeholder confirmation.


## Government stakeholder walkthrough

Start at `#customs`, then open **North Star**. The visit is populated so stakeholders can inspect the information and decision states without entering data.

1. **Customs file** (`#customs/gov-demo`): vessel identity, representation, voyage/declarations, supporting filenames, current cruising request, fictional assessment and team decisions. The decision controls are optional.
2. **Immigration file** (`#immigration/gov-demo`): three fictional people, captured passport details, one checked identity review and a passport expiring during the planned visit. Explain that observations and identity checks do not grant admission.
3. **Government e-pay** (`#epay/gov-demo/ASSESS-1`): review the assessment and preconfigured fictional payment method. Select a successful, declined or cancelled simulation. No real card details or money are used. A successful result produces a downloadable demo receipt.
4. Return to **Customs reconciliation**: the receipt awaits verification. The optional verification control updates the evidence; issuance stays separate in the existing assessment/issuance desk.
5. **Public verification** (`#verify/HBR-DEMO-GOV`) shows the scoped process outcomes without passport identities, private review notes or receipts.

Navigation keeps session changes; reloading restores the examples. Planned visits are January 2027. The assessment date uses the current Anguilla date. The demonstration amount is illustrative, not a government fee quote.
